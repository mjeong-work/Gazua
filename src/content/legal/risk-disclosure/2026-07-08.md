## 1. Investing Involves Risk

All investing carries risk, including the possible loss of some or all of the money invested. There is no guarantee that any investment strategy, asset, or approach discussed on Gazua will be profitable or suitable for you.

## 2. Past Performance Does Not Guarantee Future Results

Historical performance, backtests, or track records referenced on Gazua — whether shared by creators or generated by our tools — reflect the past. Markets change, and past results are not a reliable predictor of what will happen next.

## 3. Volatility & Market Risk

Prices for stocks, cryptocurrencies, and other assets can be highly volatile and can decline sharply and unpredictably, including as a result of factors unrelated to any specific asset (broad market conditions, interest rates, geopolitical events, and more).

## 4. Concentration & Leverage Risk

Strategies discussed on Gazua may involve concentrated positions, leverage, options, or other approaches that carry greater risk than a diversified, unleveraged portfolio. Understand any strategy fully — including its downside — before considering it.

## 5. Cryptocurrency-Specific Risk

Cryptocurrency assets are especially volatile, are not backed by any government, and may be subject to limited regulatory oversight compared to traditional securities. Additional risks include exchange failures, custody risk, and irreversible transactions.

## 6. Conduct Your Own Research

Always conduct your own research and, where appropriate, consult a licensed financial, tax, or legal professional before making investment decisions. Nothing on Gazua is personalized to your financial situation. See our [Investment Disclaimer](/legal/investment-disclaimer) for more.
