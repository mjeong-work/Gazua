## 1. Respecting Intellectual Property

Gazua respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users and creators to do the same. We respond to valid copyright infringement notices in accordance with the Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA).

## 2. Filing a Takedown Notice

If you believe content on Gazua infringes your copyright, send a written notice to [dmca@gazua.com](mailto:dmca@gazua.com) that includes:

- A description of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed
- The specific URL or location of the material on Gazua
- Your contact information (name, address, phone number, email)
- A statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law
- A statement, made under penalty of perjury, that the information in the notice is accurate and that you are authorized to act on behalf of the copyright owner
- Your physical or electronic signature

## 3. What Happens Next

Upon receiving a valid notice, we will remove or disable access to the identified content and notify the user or creator who posted it. Repeat infringers may have their accounts suspended or terminated.

## 4. Filing a Counter-Notice

If your content was removed and you believe it was removed in error, you may submit a counter-notice to [dmca@gazua.com](mailto:dmca@gazua.com) with your contact information, an identification of the removed content, and a statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the content was removed by mistake or misidentification. We may restore the content after a statutory waiting period unless the original complainant files a legal action.

## 5. Your Own Content

By posting content on Gazua, you confirm that you own it or have the right to share it. See our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) for the license you grant us and the license you retain.
