## 1. Our Goal

Gazua works because people share real research and honest perspectives. These guidelines exist to keep discussion useful, honest, and safe — for people at every experience level.

## 2. Share Research, Not Recommendations

Post what you've learned, how you're thinking about something, or what you're watching — not instructions. Avoid phrasing that tells others what to do with their money ("buy now," "sell everything," "this is a guaranteed win").

## 3. Be Honest About What You Don't Know

It's fine to be a beginner and say so. It's not fine to present speculation as certainty. If you're not sure about something, say that — the community is more useful when confidence matches conviction.

## 4. No Guaranteed Returns or Manipulation

The following are never allowed on Gazua:

- Promising or implying guaranteed profits or specific returns
- Coordinating group buying or selling of a specific security ("pump" activity)
- Undisclosed paid promotion of a stock, coin, or fund
- Impersonating another person, creator, or company
- Fabricated screenshots, fake performance data, or doctored charts

## 5. Disclose What's Relevant

If you hold a position in something you're posting about, say so. If you're being compensated to talk about something, disclose it. Our publishing flow will prompt you for these disclosures — please answer them honestly. See our [Creator Policy](/legal/creator-policy) for creator-specific disclosure requirements.

## 6. Respectful Disagreement

Investing opinions differ, and that's the point. Disagree with ideas, not people. Harassment, hate speech, and personal attacks aren't tolerated, regardless of how confident you are that you're right.

## 7. Reporting & Enforcement

You can report any post, reel, or comment that violates these guidelines. Our team reviews reports and may warn, remove content, or suspend accounts that repeatedly or seriously violate these rules. See our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms) for the full enforcement framework.

## 8. Contact

Questions about these guidelines can be sent to [legal@gazua.com](mailto:legal@gazua.com).
