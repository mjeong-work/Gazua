## 1. Why This Policy Exists

Good research depends on trustworthy sources. This policy explains where the data on Gazua comes from, and what we expect when creators or users cite outside sources.

## 2. Market Data

Real-time and historical market data displayed on Gazua is provided by third-party data vendors (including Polygon.io). We do not guarantee this data is free of delay, error, or omission — always confirm time-sensitive figures with your brokerage or an official source before acting on them.

## 3. Source-First Research

We encourage — and where possible, our tools prompt for — linking to original, publicly available sources: company filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K), official press releases, regulatory filings, and primary reporting. Secondhand summaries (including AI-generated ones) should be treated as a starting point for research, not a substitute for the original source.

## 4. Citing Sources

When sharing research that relies on a specific data point, claim, or figure, cite where it came from wherever practical. This helps readers verify claims themselves rather than taking them on faith — a core part of how we think research should work on Gazua. See our [Research Integrity Principles](/legal/research-integrity-principles).

## 5. Data We Don't Verify

Gazua does not independently audit or verify user- or creator-submitted data, screenshots, or performance claims. Treat unverified figures with appropriate skepticism, and report content that appears to misrepresent data.

## 6. AI-Sourced Summaries

Some content on Gazua is generated by AI models summarizing publicly available information. AI-generated summaries may misattribute, omit, or misstate source material. See our [AI Policy](/legal/ai-policy) for how we handle AI-generated content.
