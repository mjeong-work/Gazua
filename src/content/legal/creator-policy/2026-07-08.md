## 1. Who This Applies To

This policy applies to anyone publishing creator content on Gazua — profile posts, videos, reels, and investment models — in addition to our general [Community Guidelines](/legal/community-guidelines).

## 2. Your Opinions Are Your Own

Everything you publish as a creator is your independent opinion and research. It does not represent an endorsement, verification, or recommendation by Gazua. Every creator article automatically displays this to readers: *"The opinions expressed are solely those of the creator and do not represent Gazua."*

## 3. Required Disclosures

Before publishing, you'll be asked to disclose anything relevant to the content, including:

- **Position held** — you hold or recently held a position in an asset you're discussing
- **Sponsored content** — you were paid or compensated to create the content
- **Affiliate links** — the content contains links that earn you a referral commission
- **Compensation received** — you received cash, equity, or other compensation related to the content

When disclosed, these show to readers as clear labels — for example, **"Creator Position Disclosed"** or **"Sponsored Content"** — directly on the content.

## 4. What Creators May Not Do

- Present opinions as guaranteed outcomes
- Coordinate trading activity with your audience
- Accept compensation for a stock or coin mention without disclosing it
- Publish content on behalf of an issuer without disclosing the relationship

## 5. Monetization & Subscriptions

Creators may offer paid tiers or subscriber-only content through Gazua. Monetization does not change your disclosure obligations — sponsored or compensated content must always be labeled, regardless of who's paying.

## 6. Accuracy & Corrections

If you discover an error in something you've published, correct or update it as soon as reasonably possible. Repeated, uncorrected inaccuracies — especially around specific securities — may result in content removal.

## 7. Enforcement

Violations of this policy are handled under the same review process described in our [Terms of Service](/legal/terms), and may include content removal, loss of creator status, or account suspension for repeated or serious violations.

## 8. Contact

Creator policy questions can be sent to [creators@gazua.com](mailto:creators@gazua.com).
