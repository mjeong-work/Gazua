This page isn't a legal agreement — there's nothing to sign or accept here. It's a plain description of the principles that shape how Gazua is built and how we hope the community researches and discusses investing.

## Transparency

We label what's AI-generated, what's user-generated, and what's sponsored. We disclose when creators hold positions or receive compensation. Nothing on Gazua should look more official, more verified, or more authoritative than it actually is.

## Education Over Recommendation

Gazua exists to help you think better about investing, not to tell you what to buy. Our tools, our creators, and our product decisions are all built around that distinction — surfacing research and perspectives, never instructions.

## Source-First Research

Good conclusions come from good sources. We encourage linking to original, publicly available information — filings, official data, primary reporting — over secondhand summaries, and we treat AI-generated content as a starting point for research, never the final word. See our [Data & Source Policy](/legal/data-source-policy).

## Independent Thinking

The most useful thing you can take from Gazua is a sharper way of evaluating ideas — not a specific idea to copy. We'd rather you disagree with a creator for a good reason than agree with one for no reason.

## Responsible AI

AI can accelerate research, but it can also be confidently wrong. We build AI features to be clearly labeled, easy to verify, and easy to report — and we say so plainly when something might be inaccurate. See our [AI Policy](/legal/ai-policy).

## Respectful Community

Investing opinions will always differ, and that tension is useful when it's handled well. We aim for a community where people can disagree about ideas without being disrespectful to each other.
