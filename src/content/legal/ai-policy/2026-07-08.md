## 1. How We Use AI

Gazua uses AI models (including Anthropic's Claude) to help you research faster — for example, generating personalized investment-style summaries, surfacing trends in your activity, and assisting with content discovery. AI is a research tool on Gazua, not a decision-maker.

## 2. AI-Generated Content May Be Wrong

AI models can produce inaccurate, incomplete, outdated, or misleading output — a phenomenon sometimes called "hallucination." This applies to any AI-generated content on Gazua, including insights, summaries, and suggestions.

We label AI-generated content when you see it. Always verify anything important using original, publicly available sources before relying on it — especially before making a financial decision.

## 3. AI Content Is Not Investment Advice

No AI output on Gazua constitutes investment advice, and no AI feature is a substitute for your own judgment or for consulting a licensed professional. See our [Investment Disclaimer](/legal/investment-disclaimer) for more.

## 4. What Data AI Features Use

Our AI features may use your on-platform activity (such as posts you've engaged with, watchlist items, and stated interests from onboarding) to generate personalized output. We do not share your personal data with AI providers beyond what's needed to generate that specific response. See our [Privacy Policy](/legal/privacy) for details on data handling.

## 5. Human Oversight

Gazua does not treat AI output as final. Community-facing AI features are designed to be reviewed, correctable, and reportable — if you believe AI-generated content is inaccurate or harmful, you can report it the same way you'd report any other content on the platform.

## 6. Changes to AI Features

AI models and the way we use them evolve quickly. We may update, replace, or retire AI features over time, and we'll update this policy when the substance of how we use AI materially changes.

## 7. Contact

Questions about our AI features or this policy can be sent to [legal@gazua.com](mailto:legal@gazua.com).
